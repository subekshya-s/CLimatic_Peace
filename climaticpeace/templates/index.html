About the Project

Bridging Faith & Climate Action

A transformative 12-month initiative uniting young people from diverse faith traditions to lead real change across 5 countries.

Our Mission

Empowering Youth for Change

To empower youth of faith aged 18–30 through training, mentoring, and resources that enable them to create impactful projects addressing climate change and promoting sustainability, while emphasizing the importance of interfaith dialogue and collaboration.


We believe that young people, guided by the values of their faith, have the power to become the most effective agents of environmental stewardship in their communities.

Our Vision

To cultivate a generation of young leaders who champion climate action and sustainable development, fostering unity and respect among diverse faith communities, ultimately paving the way for a harmonious and resilient world.

Project Overview

Youth Are Green Faith Builders 2 empowers young individuals from diverse faith backgrounds to become proactive leaders in climate action. Through comprehensive training, mentoring, and funding, participants develop initiatives promoting intercultural dialogue and sustainable livelihoods — aligned with SDG 13, SDG 5, and SDG 9.

Program Phases

A Journey of Growth & Impact

Six phases from August 2024 to May 2025.

  1. 01

    Preparation & Partnership Building

    Aug – Sep 2024

    Building partnerships with faith organizations, NGOs, and stakeholders.

  2. 02

    Outreach & Program Launch

    October 2024

    Spreading awareness and inviting youth from five countries.

  3. 03

    Camp Phase

    November 2024

    3-day intensive training on faith, climate, entrepreneurship, and peace.

  4. 04

    Mentoring Phase

    December 2024

    Mentorship from experts across multiple countries.

  5. 05

    Implementation of Initiatives

    Jan – Apr 2025

    Youth-led community projects creating real-world impact.

  6. 06

    Evaluation & Impact

    May 2025

    Measuring impact and showcasing achievements.

What You Gain

Benefits for Participants

Real resources, real mentors, and a real global network.

Project Funding

Receive financial support to implement your community climate initiative, awarded after the camp based on your proposal.

Expert Mentorship

Paired with mentors from four countries for personalized guidance, regular check-ins, and hands-on workshops.

Certification

Official certificate recognizing your training in climate action, peace leadership, and social entrepreneurship.

Global Network

Connect with 100+ youth leaders and international partners across Lebanon, Jordan, Tunisia, Albania, and Indonesia.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility & Expectations

You should be:

  • Between 18 and 30 years old (required)
  • Enrolled in or completed an undergraduate degree
  • Interested in faith, environment, or relevant fields
  • Have relevant work or volunteer experience
  • Have an established local network or willingness to build one
  • Fluent in English with good communication skills
  • Keen on the interplay of religious beliefs and environmental stewardship

What we expect from you:

  • Run a workshop for at least 50 people in your community
  • Commit to a 3-month project focused on local or national impact
  • Participate for the full duration including pre and post sessions
  • Demonstrate eagerness to learn and expand your network
  • Show commitment to becoming a leader in your community
